## 📊 Human Resources Analytics Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing a Human Resources dataset to gain insights into staff distribution, salary structure, promotions, and workforce demographics.
The analysis was visualized using Power BI, providing clear and interactive insights to support HR decision-making.

The dashboard answers key business questions such as staff strength, salary spending, promotions, departmental performance, and gender distribution.

---

##🗂 Dataset Description

The dataset contains employee-level HR information with the following columns:

## 📂 Dataset Description

*Dataset Name:* HR_data.csv  

| Column Name | Description |
|--------------|-------------|
| StaffID | Unique identifier for each staff |
| Staff_Name | Name of staff |

| Department | Various depart of staff |
| Date_of_Appointment | Staff Date of apointment |
| Salary | Staff salary |
| Promotion | Promotion status |
| Date_of_Promotion | Staff promotion date |
| City | staff city of residence |

## 🎯 Objectives / Business Questions Answered

The analysis answers the following questions:

How many staff do we have?

What is the average salary?

What is the maximum salary?

What is the minimum salary?

How much does the company spend on salaries every month?

How many staff were promoted in 2021 and 2022?

How many promotions occurred based on department?

Which city has the highest total salary?

Which city has the lowest total salary?

Which department earns the highest salary?

Which department has the oldest staff (longest years of service)?

How many staff are there based on gender?
---
## 📈 Key Insights

The organization has 100 total staff.

The average salary is approximately $59,050.

The maximum salary recorded is $79,900, while the minimum salary is $40,030.

The company spends approximately $5.9 million monthly on staff salaries.

Promotions occurred across 2021 and 2022, with higher promotion activity in 2022.

Marketing and Engineering departments show strong salary and service-year contributions.

Chicago has the highest total salary payout among cities, while Los Angeles records the lowest.

Salary distribution by gender shows a balanced workforce (50% Male, 50% Female).
